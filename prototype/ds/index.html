Grove

The design system of Open Campus. Everything here exists because a lesson is a branch you grow.

One idea, used everywhere

Each episode you finish draws the line to the next. Coming back makes the line thicker. Colour, shape and motion all serve that.

What follows from it

The arch is the frame for anything you watch. The branch is how progress is shown. Coral is the only signal colour and it marks exactly two things: the node of the logo and what you have grown. Light is for reading, deep conifer is for watching and navigating.

Motion

Three durations, three easings. Only opacity, transform and stroke move. Reduced motion turns all of it off.

Tokens

--dur-micro 120ms for hover and press. --dur-short 220ms for state changes. --dur-long 420ms for the branch growing and the episode cross-fade. --ease-out for almost everything, --ease-in-out for loops.

The branch grows

Finishing an episode draws the vine to the next leaf. Replaying a lesson thickens it, up to four times.

      Rules

      Do

      • Use coral only for growth and the logo node.
      • Show progress as the branch, not as a bar.
      • Keep text in plain words before technical ones.
      • Give every control a visible focus ring.
      • Pair a colour change with a shape change.

      Do not

      • Fill buttons with coral.
      • Use red for a wrong answer.
      • Add a second accent colour.
      • Animate width, height, top or left.
      • Move anything when reduced motion is on.
